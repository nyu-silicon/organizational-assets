About

Next Gen Compute brings together people curious about how computers work at every layer — from transistors to architectures to the software that runs on them. We host talks, workshops, and hands-on projects.

Focus areas

  • Hardware

    Chip design, digital logic, and silicon.

  • Architecture

    Accelerators, parallelism, and new compute paradigms.

  • Systems

    Compilers, runtimes, and the software stack.
