A student club exploring chips, systems, and the ideas that will power computing's future.

about

We bring together people curious about how computers work at every layer, from transistors to architectures to the software that runs on them. We host talks, workshops, and hands-on projects.

focus

  • hardware: chip design, digital logic, silicon
  • architecture: accelerators, parallelism, new compute paradigms
  • systems: compilers, runtimes, the software stack

join

Everyone is welcome, no experience required. Reach out to get on the mailing list.
